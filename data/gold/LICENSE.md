# License and rights notice

## This benchmark

The gold transcriptions (`*_gold.txt`), bake-off table, and packaging of this
dataset were prepared for the gojri-ai project. They are shared to support
research and to help Gojri show up in AI and NLP work.

## Page images

Images are short page excerpts from printed Gojri books used for OCR testing
(dictionaries, poetry, textbooks, folklore materials). Those books remain under
their original copyright. This release is **not** permission to redistribute the
full books.

## Please

1. Cite this dataset if you use it.
2. Respect the original books and their authors/publishers.
3. Treat results as experimental. Spot-check before trusting OCR output.
