## What this dataset is

A **script-conversion derivative**: Devanagari headwords from the source were
converted to Gojri Nastaliq (Perso-Arabic) forms, with optional Roman and short
English gloss snippets carried from the same headword lines for reference.

This release does **not** include:

- the full dictionary PDF
- full page prose, examples, or literary citations from the book
- the FLI Gojri Literature Corpus
- Mozilla Common Voice audio
