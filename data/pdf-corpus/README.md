# Source books: credits and rights notice

The Gojri print books that this project works on are third-party works. This folder holds their credits and the rights
notice. **It does not hold the books.**

## Status

The project once kept a Hugging Face collection of the book PDFs (`v1.0`, 74 files, then `v2.0`, 64 files, 14,518
pages). It has been **private since 2026-11-23**, because the project has no written permission to redistribute the
books. Nothing in this repository gives access to it. Get each book from its original source.

## Rights

Copyright of every book stays with its authors, editors and publishers. The project is non-profit and non-commercial.

- Read [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md) and [`CREDITS.tsv`](CREDITS.tsv).
- The credit lines are helpers. The title page of each book is the authority.
- Text that the project shares from a book (OCR text, dictionary text, the lexicon, page excerpts) names its source book.
- Authors or publishers who want a text or an excerpt removed can open an issue on
  [github.com/jaslam94/gojri-ai](https://github.com/jaslam94/gojri-ai) or contact the maintainer.

## Files here

- `CREDITS.tsv`: one row per book: file name, page count, scope, credit line, notes and MD5 hash (of the private copy).
- `LICENSE.md`: usage terms and copyright notice.

## Credits

Principal author and editor credits across the books include:

- Dr. Javaid Rahi (editor and compiler of Gojri dictionaries, folklore anthologies and poetry collections)
- Prof. (Dr.) Rafique Anjum and co-authors (dictionary and Anjum Shanasi publications)
- Other authors and publishers, listed in `CREDITS.tsv` and on the printed title pages

## Accuracy and AI use

The catalog, the duplicate checks and the credit lines were made with AI-assisted scripts. We check our figures against
the data, but errors can remain. Corrections are welcome: see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
